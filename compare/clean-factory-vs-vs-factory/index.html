Clean Factory vs VS Factory: Which Makes the Best Super Clone Rolex?

A comprehensive engineering teardown comparing case tolerances, movement calibers, crystal transparency, and ceramic sintering between Guangdong's two premier replica manufacturers.

Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): The Verdict

Choose VS Factory (VSF) if your priority is the Rolex Submariner (126610LN / 116610LN) or Datejust 41, because VSF holds the exclusive contract for the true Dandong 3235 movement (72-hour power reserve) and provides superior synthetic sapphire crystal clarity with zero milky ring distortion.

Choose Clean Factory if you are purchasing a Rolex GMT-Master II (Pepsi, Batman, Sprite) or Cosmograph Daytona, where Clean dominates with the industry's finest two-tone Cerachrom ceramic bezels and Dandong 3285 / 4130 integrated column-wheel calibers.

Why VS Factory Dominates the Datejust & Submariner

VS Factory holds an exclusive exclusivity contract with the Dandong movement factory for the 3235 caliber. The Dandong 3235 features genuine cloned Paraflex shock absorbers, a balance wheel regulating bridge with movable stud holder, and a full 72-hour extended mainspring barrel. When winding a VSF 3235, the tactile resistance feels remarkably smooth and fluid—identical to genuine Rolex 3235 calibers.

Why Clean Factory Dominates GMT-Master II & Daytona

Clean Factory began as a specialized ceramic bezel insert manufacturer before assembling complete timepieces. Clean perfected the notoriously difficult transition point on the bi-color Cerachrom ceramic bezel for the GMT-Master II (such as the red-to-blue seam on reference 126710BLRO). Furthermore, Clean's Dandong 4130 Daytona case maintains the genuine 12.2mm thickness with fully operational chronograph complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VS Factory or Clean Factory better for a Rolex Submariner?

For black dial Submariners (126610LN or 116610LN), VS Factory is the widely recognized community benchmark because of its crystal clarity and 72-hour Dandong 3235 movement. For the green bezel 'Starbucks' (126610LV), Clean Factory is frequently favored for its closer match to the OEM ceramic green hue.

Who makes the best Rolex GMT-Master II Super Clone?

Clean Factory is the clear industry leader for the GMT-Master II collection. Equipped with the Dandong 3285 movement, it provides true independent jumping hour functionality, allowing seamless time zone adjustment without stopping the balance wheel.
