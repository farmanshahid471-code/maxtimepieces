Super Clone vs Genuine Rolex Submariner: 1:1 Side-by-Side Teardown

A precise physical teardown examining the reference 126610LN Submariner Date: micrometer case dimensions, 904L metallurgy density, platinum-sputtered ceramic bezels, and cyclops anti-reflective performance.

Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): Can You Tell the Difference?

On the wrist, a top-tier VS Factory (VSF) or Clean Factory 1:1 Super Clone Submariner is indistinguishable from a genuine reference 126610LN to the naked eye. The Super Clone weighs 155 grams (exact match with genuine within ±2g), utilizes genuine solid 904L Oystersteel, features platinum PVD Cerachrom numerals, and houses a 72-hour cloned Dandong 3235 movement. Only high-powered 40x macro inspection of the hairspring stud carrier or escapement wheel separates them.

1. The 904L Steel Graining

Both cases feature identical radial brushing along the upper lug surface that tapers seamlessly into mirror-polished case flanks. The high nickel/molybdenum concentration in the 904L alloy ensures a distinctive cool, silvery-white luster that standard 316L cannot replicate.

2. Laser-Etched Rehaut

Around the inner stainless steel bezel wall (the rehaut), "ROLEXROLEXROLEX" is double-etched with exact font kerning. The Rolex crown logo at 12 o'clock aligns directly with the center of the 12 o'clock dial hour marker.

3. Glidelock Clasp Mechanics

The Oysterlock safety clasp contains a fully operational Glidelock fine-adjustment extension system, permitting tool-free adjustments in 2mm increments up to 20mm total length to comfortably fit over wetsuits or adjust during warm weather.
