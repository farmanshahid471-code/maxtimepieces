Service Guidelines

Shipping Policy & Delivery Information

Last updated: November 2026

1. Order Processing & Quality Control (QC)

Every timepiece curated by Max Timepieces undergoes a rigorous multi-point mechanical and cosmetic inspection prior to packaging. Quality Control (QC) documentation—including high-definition macro photography and live electronic timegrapher testing videos—is generated and shared directly with the client via WhatsApp (+1 571 661 4938) within 2 to 4 business days of order confirmation. Dispatch occurs only after client QC approval.

2. Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

Once QC is approved, shipments are securely packaged in shock-absorbent, tamper-evident protective transit casing and handed over to premium international courier carriers (e.g., FedEx, DHL Express, Royal Mail, USPS priority network). Estimated delivery transit windows are:

  • United States & Canada: 7 to 12 business days following dispatch.
  • United Kingdom & European Union: 7 to 14 business days following dispatch.
  • Australia, UAE & Worldwide: 8 to 15 business days following dispatch.

3. Shipment Tracking

Clients receive an active tracking number via WhatsApp and email as soon as the package is processed by the air carrier. Online tracking updates typically populate within 48 hours of transit scan.

4. Customs & Import Duties

All shipments are processed with lawful, standard customs paperwork appropriate for international horological accessories and timekeeping instruments. Customers are responsible for any applicable local import duties or clearance fees imposed by their national customs authority.

5. Lost or Damaged In Transit Protection

If a parcel is confirmed lost in transit by the carrier or sustains physical transit damage prior to delivery, Max Timepieces coordinates an immediate investigation and provides a replacement dispatch at no additional cost to the client upon carrier verification.

6. Contact Support

For live shipping inquiries or transit updates, contact our concierge directly on WhatsApp: +1 (571) 661-4938 or via our Contact Page.
