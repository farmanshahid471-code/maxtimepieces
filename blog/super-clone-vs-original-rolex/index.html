Super Clone vs Original: Metallurgy, Movements & Micro-Tolerances

A micrometer and metallurgical inspection.

How close does modern super clone manufacturing actually come to authentic luxury Swiss manufacture? To answer this objectively, our horology team performed an exhaustive teardown comparing case weights, alloy compositions, crystal anti-reflective properties, and timegrapher readings.

1. Solid 904L Oystersteel vs 316L

Standard replicas utilize common 316L stainless steel. By contrast, top-tier super clones are CNC milled from solid bars of 904L Oystersteel—an alloy high in nickel and chromium that gives the metal a distinctive warm silvery luster and superior saltwater corrosion resistance.

2. Case Tolerances & Weight

Using digital micrometers, modern super clone cases match authentic dimensions within ±0.05mm. Total sized watch weight on an Oystersteel bracelet matches genuine references within ±2 grams (e.g., 154g vs 155g on a 41mm Submariner).

3. Timegrapher Rate & Amplitude

Equipped with Dandong 3235 and 4130 calibers, regulated super clones achieve daily rate deviations between -3 to +5 seconds per day, with amplitudes resting stably between 275° and 310°.
