Dandong 3235, 4130, 4131 & 3285: Complete Rolex Clone Movement Guide

An engineering deep-dive into Dandong movement architecture, true power reserve capabilities, gear train geometry, and regulation tolerances.

Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): Cloned Caliber Summary

The era of decorative plate replicas is over. Modern 1:1 Super Clones use standalone cloned mechanical architectures manufactured by Liaoning Dandong Watch Co. The Dandong 3235 delivers a true 72-hour power reserve, while the Dandong 4130 is a fully integrated column-wheel chronograph with zero non-functional dummy parts. For dual-timezone GMTs, the Dandong 3285 provides a genuine independent jumping local hour hand.

1. Dandong 3235 (VS Factory Exclusive)

Engineered for: Rolex Submariner Date (126610), Datejust 41 (126334), Sea-Dweller

72-Hour Reserve

Technical Specs:

  • • Frequency: 28,800 vph (4 Hz)
  • • Shock System: Cloned Paraflex
  • • Winding: Bidirectional rotor
  • • Date Mechanism: Instantaneous snap at 12:00

Engineering Analysis:

The Dandong 3235 is regarded as one of the greatest milestones in cloned horology. Unlike previous Asian 2824 or 2836 movements that added decorative plates on top of stock movements, the Dandong 3235 is built from the ground up to match the Rolex Calibre 3235. The balance wheel regulating bridge, stud carrier, and balance spring position match OEM schematics, allowing genuine balance staff and wheel replacements if needed.

2. Dandong 4130 / 4131 (Clean & BT Factory)

Engineered for: Rolex Cosmograph Daytona (116500LN / 126500LN)

Integrated Column Wheel

Technical Specs:

  • • Architecture: Integrated Chronograph
  • • Clutch: Vertical friction clutch
  • • Power Reserve: ~70 Hours
  • • Case Thickness: 12.2mm true to genuine

Engineering Analysis:

Chronograph movements are traditionally vulnerable in replicas. Early versions used Asian 7750 movements with gear transfer plates that caused running second hands to stutter and increased case thickness to 14.5mm. The Dandong 4130 solved this permanently by replicating the genuine column wheel mechanism and vertical clutch, reducing part friction and delivering ultra-smooth pusher engagement.

3. Dandong 3285 (Clean Factory Exclusive)

Engineered for: Rolex GMT-Master II (126710BLRO Pepsi, 126710BLNR Batman, 126720VTNR Sprite)

Jumping Hour Hand

Technical Specs:

  • • Complication: True GMT with jumping hour
  • • Hand Stack: Hour, GMT, Minute, Second (Correct Hand Stack)
  • • Power Reserve: ~70 Hours
  • • Date Setting: Forward & backward hour jump

Engineering Analysis:

The breakthrough of the Dandong 3285 is its genuine jumping hour functionality. When crossing time zones, pulling the crown to position 2 allows the hour hand to jump forward or backward in clean 1-hour increments without disrupting the continuous operation of the minute hand or balance wheel. Furthermore, the 24-hour red GMT hand operates independently, pointing directly to home time against the rotating ceramic bezel.
